Who We Are

Wisdom in Care. Excellence in Service.

Sunesis comes from the Greek word for wisdom, the critical faculty of understanding and comprehension, and that is the heart of how we approach healthcare. At Sunesis Medical Services, we believe that true well-being means looking at the whole person, not just treating symptoms.

We are a dynamic medical company dedicated to providing comprehensive, compassionate, and convenient healthcare. Our goal is simple: to meet you exactly where you are, whether at home, at work, in school, or on the move.

Dr. Joel Omoding
Chief Executive Officer

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality medical services that empower individuals, families, and organizations to achieve and maintain good health. We are committed to delivering care with professionalism, empathy, and integrity.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of holistic, mobile, and people-centered medical solutions, setting a new standard for convenient and compassionate healthcare in our communities.

Our Approach

Care for the Whole Person

We go beyond treating symptoms. True well-being lives at the meeting point of three things we care for together.

Body

Physical health through doctor consultations, home nursing, mobile laboratory services, and ongoing chronic disease management, delivered where you are most comfortable.

Mind

Mental and emotional well-being through confidential counseling, professional therapy, and supportive care, because emotional health matters just as much as physical health.

Environment

The setting you heal in. By bringing care to your home, workplace, or school, we let you recover in the surroundings where you feel safest and most at ease.
