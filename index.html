About Sunesis

Wisdom in Care. Excellence in Service.

Sunesis comes from the Greek word for wisdom, the critical faculty of understanding and comprehension, and that is the heart of how we approach healthcare. We are a dynamic medical company delivering comprehensive, compassionate, and convenient healthcare directly to individuals, families, schools, and organizations. Our approach goes beyond treating symptoms, we care for the whole person: body, mind, and environment.
