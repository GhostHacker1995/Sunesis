First Aid Services & Training

First Aid That's Ready Before You Need It

Emergencies don't wait for a hospital to be nearby. We provide certified first aid and CPR training for individuals, schools, and workplaces, along with on-site first aid coverage for events and organizations, so the people around you are ready to respond when it matters most.

Book First Aid Training Request Event Coverage
What's Included

Practical, hands-on training

  • Certified Basic First Aid training courses, for individuals and groups
  • CPR and AED (defibrillator) training
  • Workplace First Aid Officer certification, designed to meet occupational health and safety requirements for businesses
  • School first aid training for teachers, administrators, and support staff
  • On-site first aid coverage and standby medical support for events, conferences, and organizational functions
  • Corporate group training packages, scheduled on-site at your workplace
  • Certificates of completion issued to all trainees
Who It's For

Be ready when it matters most

Schools building staff capacity to respond to student emergencies, companies meeting workplace safety and compliance requirements, event organizers who need standby medical coverage, and individuals who simply want the skills and confidence to respond in an emergency.

Why It Matters

A trained responder changes the outcome

A trained first responder in the room, the office, or the classroom can be the difference between a manageable situation and a crisis. Our training is practical, hands-on, and built around real scenarios our trainees are likely to actually face.
